🌟 What Is This?

Have you ever wondered where you went this year? Or wanted to see a visual map of all your travels? google-timeline-visualizer is a simple, powerful tool that takes your Google Location History (also known as Google Timeline) and turns it into a beautiful, easy-to-understand map.

Instead of scrolling through endless lists of dates and coordinates, you'll see your entire year laid out visually. You can see every city you visited, every road you drove, and every path you walked—all in one glance. It's like a personal travel diary made of maps!

This app is designed for anyone who uses Google Maps on their phone and wants to relive their adventures. No technical knowledge is needed—just download, run, and watch your year unfold.

✨ Key Features

  • 📊 Interactive Yearly Map: See all your locations plotted on a dynamic map for any selected year.
  • 🛤️ Travel Routes: Visualize the actual paths you took between cities and countries.
  • 🏙️ City & Country Stats: Discover how many places you visited and how much time you spent in each.
  • 📅 Month-by-Month Breakdown: Drill down to see your movements month by month.
  • 💾 Export & Share: Save your maps as images to share with friends and family.
  • 🎨 Clean & Simple Interface: No confusing menus—just your data, beautifully presented.

💻 What You Need

  • 🖥️ Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit recommended)
  • 🧠 Memory: At least 4 GB of RAM
  • 💾 Storage: 200 MB of free space
  • 🌐 Internet: Required for initial download and map tiles

Step 3: Get Your Google Data

To use the app, you'll need your Google Location History file:

  1. Go to Google Takeout and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click "Deselect all" to avoid downloading everything.
  3. Scroll down and check the box next to "Location History" (sometimes called "Timeline").
  4. Click "Next step" and choose your preferred export size (smaller is fine).
  5. Click "Create export". Google will email you when it's ready—usually within minutes.
  6. Download the ZIP file and extract it to a folder on your computer.
  7. In the app, click "Load Data" and select the extracted folder or the Records.json file inside.

📖 How to Use the App

Using the app is as easy as 1-2-3:

  1. Load Your Data: Click the "Load Data" button and select your Google Takeout folder or the Records.json file.
  2. Choose a Year: Use the dropdown menu to pick the year you want to visualize.
  3. Explore! Your map will appear instantly. Zoom in and out, hover over points to see details, and use the sidebar to see your stats.

You can also use the Export button to save your map as a PNG image to share with friends or keep as a keepsake.

🛠️ Troubleshooting & Tips

  • 🔍 No data showing? Make sure Location History is enabled on your phone (Settings → Location → Google Location History).
  • 📁 Can't find your file? After extracting the ZIP from Google Takeout, look for a folder named Takeout and inside it, Location History.
  • 🐢 App is slow? Large data files can take a moment to load. Be patient—it's processing thousands of points!
  • 🔄 Windows SmartScreen warning? If you see a blue popup saying "Windows protected your PC," click "More info" then "Run anyway". This is normal for new apps.
  • 📅 Missing older years? Google only keeps Location History from when you enabled it. If you don't see 2019, you didn't have it on then.
